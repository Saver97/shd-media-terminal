# Third-party components

The portable build contains Python, PySide6/Qt, Shiboken, Requests and their dependencies. Their original license files are included under `third-party-licenses/`; the project MIT license does not replace them.

Qt libraries are dynamically linked and may be replaced with compatible builds. Qt/PySide6 version: 6.11.2. Source: https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/ and https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/ . Qt WebEngine Chromium notices: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtwebengine-licensing.html . Python source and license: https://www.python.org/downloads/source/ . PyInstaller: https://pyinstaller.org/ .
